About the game

THE MEMORY ATLAS is an original point-and-click adventure for the browser. You play Elias Vane, a young scholar who can, after a first long night at Greyhaven, look at a fountain, a watch or a locked door and see the memory still clinging to it.

Three chapters — The Things We Forget, The Lost Collection, and The Underarchives — make up one story. Talk to Mira Thorne, the librarian Wren, Professor Ashcroft and others. Combine scraps of a founders’ nocturne, open a stair beneath the courtyard fountain, and chase the Archivist for the stolen leaves of a book that was meant to hold every memory ever lived. Your progress is saved on your device. A notebook and a hint button keep you on the thread if you wander.

Controls

Explore

Tap a marker to look, talk or take. Drag the scene, or use the side arrows, to look around. Elias walks to whatever you choose. Play in landscape when you can.

Satchel, vision, hints

Tap an item to use it, tap it again to examine it, or combine two things from the satchel. The eye toggles Memory Vision — then tap anything that glows. The book is Elias’s notebook; the question mark is a hint. Fullscreen sits in the top-right corner, or add ?fs=1 to the play address.
