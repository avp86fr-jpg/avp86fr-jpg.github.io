About the game

BULLET TIME is an original arcade light-gun shooter you can play in the browser. Aim down a dusty 1881 main street, a 1929 speakeasy block, a feudal keep, a Roman arena, a pirate deck, a foggy London, a moonlit necropolis, a neon 1986 boardwalk, a chrome future, and the end of time itself.

Hostiles pop from cover. Civilians wander the street — hitting one hurts your grade. Fill the Bullet Time meter and slow the world when the heat is on. After most eras, a hidden-object investigation turns up the next lead. A hypercar bonus stage breaks up the campaign, and Daily is a short run of its own.

Features

  • 10 story eras + a hypercar bonus
  • Hidden-object investigations
  • Bullet Time slow-mo
  • Cover, reload, combos and ranks
  • Rookie, Detective and Hard-Boiled
  • Daily run and a local top-10
  • Desktop mouse or phone (landscape)
  • Free in the browser

Controls

Desktop

Mouse aims, click shoots. Space, Shift or right-click takes cover and reloads. F or middle-click activates Bullet Time. P pauses. Menus take click or Space / Enter.

Phone

Play in landscape. Tap to shoot. Use the on-screen Cover and Bullet Time buttons. Pause and fullscreen sit on the HUD. Rotate if you open the game in portrait.
