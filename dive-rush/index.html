About the game

DIVE RUSH is an original diving sports game for the browser. You play the World Dive Championship: climb a cliff or a tower, hold to put your takeoff on the buoy, and in the air hold to flip and release to stretch. Hit the water straight. More flips, more points. Mistime it and you can hit the board, or the rocks.

World Tour is a season of judged events — the Amalfi Open, the Tokyo Grand Prix, the Iguazú Cup, the Arctic Masters, and a five-dive Grand Final in Paris, among others. Some events add a bullseye bonus, a star rush, a back takeoff only, or a dropped worst dive. Free Dive lets you practice any venue you have reached and chase a venue record. Your coins, medals and bests are saved on your device.

Features

  • 4 divers: Leo, Mina, Elise and Jaden, each with a unique skill
  • 10 venues on cliffs, lagoons, canyons, a fjord and indoor arenas
  • World Tour events with medals and season standings
  • Free Dive for a personal best on any unlocked board
  • Judge scores, rip entries, twists, stars and bullseyes
  • Climb from 4 m up to the high platform when you earn it
  • Original Web Audio, mute and fullscreen built in
  • Mouse, touch, or Space and Shift on a keyboard

Controls

Takeoff

Hold anywhere, Space, J or Enter to aim your jump at the buoy. Release when the marker is in the green to leap. Climb or drop a platform with the on-screen buttons. Front or back takeoff, tuck or pike, before you jump.

In the air

Hold to flip, release to stretch so you hit the water straight. Twist with the on-screen button, Shift, A, F or T. Fullscreen is the top-right corner button, the P key, or add ?fs=1 to the play address.
