About the game

GOLDEN TEN is an original track-and-field decathlon for the browser. You contest the 100 metres, long jump, shot put, high jump, 400 metres, 110m hurdles, discus, pole vault, javelin and 1500 metres. Each mark is converted with the official decathlon tables, so a sharp 100 and a big javelin both count the way they should.

Competition is a two-day meet against seven rivals at Regional, National or World level, with medals for the top three. Solo Run is just you and the ten events, chasing a best score on your device. Practice lets you retry any event on its own. The world-record mark in the game is 9126 points.

Features

  • All 10 official decathlon events
  • Real IAAF/World Athletics scoring tables
  • Competition against a field of eight, with three meet levels
  • Solo Run for a personal best across all ten
  • Practice any event, with three tries on jumps and throws
  • Stamina and rhythm on the 400 and 1500
  • Original Web Audio, mute and fullscreen built in
  • Mouse, touch, or a keyboard

Controls

Run

Tap the left half of the screen, or press Right, Left, A, D, F or J, to sprint. In the 400 and 1500, watch your stamina; in the 1500, tapping on the beat gives longer, easier strides.

Action

Hold the right half, Space, Up, K or Enter to jump, throw, plant the pole or lean at the line, then release near 45° or in the green. Fullscreen is the top-right button, the P key, or add ?fs=1 to the play address.
