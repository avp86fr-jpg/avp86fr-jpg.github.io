About the game

FROSTLINE is an original 3D downhill snowboarding game for the browser. Eight riders, each with their own speed, edge, air and balance, take on ten runs in morning light, snowfall, sunset and night. Steer, jump and grab, land clean to keep a combo, and try not to bail.

Seven modes cover a full day on the mountain: Race, Time Attack with your own ghost, Trick Attack, Slalom (a missed gate costs two seconds), Free Ride, Big Air, and Rookie, Pro and Legend Cups. Medals, records and ghosts are saved on your device.

Features

  • 8 riders with different speed, edge, air and balance
  • 10 downhill runs in changing weather and light
  • 7 modes, including cups, slalom, big air and a ghost time attack
  • Trick combos, grabs, boost and a best-trick sheet
  • Medals and personal records saved on your device
  • Original Web Audio, mute and fullscreen built in
  • Keyboard or on-screen stick, jump and grab
  • Self-contained: one file, no account, no download

Controls

Ride

Steer with Left and Right or A and D, or the on-screen stick. Pitch with Up and Down or W and S. Space or the Jump button leaves the snow. Shift, X, K or Grab holds a trick in the air.

More

Escape or P pauses. R puts you back on the track. Fullscreen is the top-right button, the F key (P is pause), or add ?fs=1 to the play address. Play in landscape when you can.
