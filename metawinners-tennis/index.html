A LITTLE COURT KNOWLEDGE

Easy to pick up. A little harder to put down.

01Choose your powerCONTROL (38%) gives a softer ball, BALANCE (68%) is your reliable rally pace, and POWER (98%) attacks. Your choice stays fixed until you change it. On a keyboard use 5, 6 or 7; the mouse wheel adjusts in 5% steps.
02Aim and play with one thumbDrag on the small court to place your target, then lift to prepare a shot. The top is deep; the bottom is near the net. This works in every direction, even near your phone’s edge. Tap a shot button to use the current target.
03One tap. One return.Assisted timing prepares one shot and waits until the ball is in reach. You can prepare while the opponent plays. SHOT READY confirms it. The shot clears after contact, a teammate’s hit, or the end of a point. Movement still matters. Choose manual timing in the pause menu for precise release timing.
PCMove, point, playWASD or arrows move; Shift adds precision. Point at the far court and click to play. Right-click cycles the selected shot. Space drives, Z adds topspin, X lobs, C slices. Q/E and R/T adjust aim; 1/2/3 choose a lane, 4 changes depth. F toggles fullscreen and Escape pauses.
PADController readyLeft stick moves, right stick aims. A drives, X adds topspin, Y lobs, B slices. Bumpers adjust power, left trigger adds precision, Start pauses.

Mobile works best in landscape. Move with the left pad and aim with your right thumb. Smart footwork follows a reachable return while you prepare; your movement always takes priority. Singles and AI doubles are first to three games, using tennis scoring and deuce. In doubles, choose an AI teammate; all four players serve in turn and the alleys count during rallies. Use Sound & Music for separate volume controls. Accessibility includes slower play, automatic returns, high-contrast visibility and reduced motion. Court surfaces change pace, bounce, spin and footing.
