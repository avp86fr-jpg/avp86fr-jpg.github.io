Landscape mobile controls

Choose Rally Touch in the lobby or match menu for assisted approach and recovery at normal movement speed. Drag the movement pad or tap your half to take over. You still select every shot and its power. Classic controls remain available, and keyboard/controller bindings remain unchanged. Move with the left thumb pad. Hold on the small court with your right thumb: the longer you hold, the more power you build. Drag to aim and release to prepare a shot. A quick tap starts at 32%; about one second reaches 100%, which stays full until you release. The top means deep, the bottom means near the net. Hold DRIVE, TOPSPIN, SLICE or LOB to build that shot’s power; drag to adjust the target, then release. Assisted timing waits for one reachable return, then clears. Manual timing is available in the pause menu. Shooting Movement lets you plant near contact, lock as soon as you press a shot, or move freely. Aim and power stay adjustable while planted.

Serving: placement, spin and second chances

The four buttons become FLAT, KICK, SLICE and SAFE while serving. Flat trades net clearance for pace; kick and safe serves give more height, while slice curves. Aim in the highlighted diagonal service box. Amber targets and net-risk messages warn before release. The serve is committed on release. A first fault gives a second serve; a double fault loses the point. A net-cord serve landing in the legal box is a let and repeats the same attempt, including on second serve. Lines count as in; doubles uses the same service boxes. Enable Guide service targets in Accessibility for safer placement. It does not remove net-risk decisions.

Keyboard and controller

WASD or arrows move; Shift adds precision. Aim at the far court with the mouse, hold to build power and release to play. Right-click cycles shot type. Hold Space for drive, Z for topspin, X for lob, or C for slice; release to play. Q/E and R/T adjust your target; 1/2/3 choose a lane and 4 changes depth. F toggles fullscreen and Escape pauses. On a compatible gamepad, the left stick moves, right stick aims, hold A/X/Y/B for drive/topspin/lob/slice to build power, then release to play.

Accessibility and assistance

Choose standard, relaxed or gentle game speed. Enable automatic returns at balanced power for reachable balls, fine aim, a larger high-contrast ball, reduced motion, or quieter court visuals. Smart footwork helps you recover while preparing a shot; manual movement takes priority until the selected shooting lock plants your feet. Switch handedness, thumb sensitivity and forgiving timing from the match menu.

Original music and court sound

Each venue has its own electronic soundtrack and atmosphere. Music builds during rallies, then settles between points. Spatial racket impacts, surface bounces, shoe steps, clay slides and crowd reactions make every exchange clearer. Open Sound & Music to mix music, game effects and ambience separately. Sound pauses with the match or when you leave the tab.

How doubles works

You control one player, with your selected AI teammate covering the other side. Two AI rivals complete the match. Rally alleys are in, serves use the diagonal service box, and all four players take turns serving. Doubles is local play against AI.
