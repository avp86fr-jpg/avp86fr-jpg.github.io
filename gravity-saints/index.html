About the game

GRAVITY SAINTS — WORLD TOUR is an original 2D physics trials game. Pick a rider, pick a sport, and take on a handmade track in the Alps, Antarctica, Patagonia, Moab, Giza, the Great Wall, the rooftops of Paris, neon Shibuya, or Sydney Harbour.

Hold a trick in the air, lean into flips, and land clean to build a combo multiplier. Grab tokens, keep your faults down, and chase bronze, silver, gold and platinum medals. A career score follows you from continent to continent, saved on your device.

Features

  • 3 sports: pedal bike, downhill ski, skateboard
  • 9 stages on 7 continents
  • 12 cel-shaded riders with unique stats
  • Tricks, flips and combo multipliers
  • Tokens, clean-run bonuses and medals
  • Checkpoints and a career best
  • Garage-rock soundtrack (Web Audio)
  • Desktop keys or on-screen touch controls

Controls

Desktop

↑ or W to go (pedal, push or tuck). ↓ or S to brake. ← → or A D to lean and spin in the air. Space to jump. Shift or X to hold a trick. R returns you to the last checkpoint (one fault). Esc or P pauses.

Phone

On-screen buttons: lean, jump, trick, brake and checkpoint. You ride forward automatically. Rotate to landscape for the best line. Mute and fullscreen sit in the corners.
