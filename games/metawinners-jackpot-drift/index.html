About the game

Bright worlds. Closer battles. Faster rivals. MetaWinners Jackpot Drift puts your character on a grid of wild tracks where the line is everything and a long drift is a personality trait.

Run a quick race or enter the Jackpot Cup. It is fan-made arcade racing, free in the browser, with a Play Fullscreen start and a corner toggle if you want to leave or re-enter.
