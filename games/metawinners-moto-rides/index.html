About the game

Own the road. METAWINNERS MOTO RIDES is a full-contact arcade motorcycle racer: 25 one-of-one riders, four circuits, and a garage that actually feels like a paddock.

This is not a gentle time trial. Riders lean, shove, and fight for the line. Fan-made, free in the browser, with a built-in fullscreen control so the circuit can fill the screen.

Features

  • 25 MetaWinners riders
  • Four circuits and a garage
  • Full-contact arcade racing
  • Built-in fullscreen and audio settings
  • Desktop and mobile

Controls

Desktop

Steer with arrows or WASD, accelerate and brake as labelled in the ride guide. F is free for the page unless you open another control.

Phone

On-screen ride controls. Use Enter Fullscreen on the settings panel or go landscape for the widest view.
