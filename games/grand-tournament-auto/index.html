About the game

Neon city, open streets, bad contracts. GRAND TOURNAMENT AUTO: Demon City is a top-down crime-action game with eight story missions, cutscenes, free roam, cars, and a demon problem that will not stay in the alley.

Fan-made and built for the browser. Drive, walk, and fight your way through a compact open world. On the keyboard, F and E are in-game actions, so the fullscreen toggle is click-only.

Features

  • 8 story missions and cutscenes
  • Free roam in a neon city
  • Cars and demon combat
  • Top-down action
  • Browser ready

Controls

Desktop

WASD to move, mouse or keys to fire and interact. F and E are used in play, so use the Full screen button instead of the F key.

Phone

On-screen stick and action buttons. Full screen plus landscape recommended.
