About the game

Grab air. Stay gold. METAWINNERS SKATE is a side-scrolling skate arcade starring four of the 1/1 crew. You ollie, flip and grind a neon city, chain JACKPOT tricks, and ride the line into a boss fight with the Gold Mage.

It is a fan-made browser game built around the MetaWinners characters, not a licensed tie-in. No wallet, no download: pick a rider and go. The soundtrack and stages are original arcade work, and the trick timing is the whole sport.

Features

  • Four playable MetaWinners riders
  • Six stages plus a Gold Mage finale
  • Ollie, flip and grind combos with JACKPOT lines
  • Keyboard and touch controls
  • Play instantly in the browser

Controls

Desktop

Arrow keys or WASD to skate, jump and trick. Follow the on-screen prompts for grinds and specials.

Phone

On-screen buttons. Landscape feels best; use Full screen to hide the browser chrome.
