About the game

A rogue AI is rewriting the clock. In HIT THE FUTURE you play CHAOS and Vera Vale across time: talk, search, and then draw when the street turns into a rail-shooter gallery.

It is a fan-made detective-shooter hybrid, playable in the browser. No download, no sign-up. When the crosshair comes out, take cover to reload and keep the timeline from folding.

Features

  • Point-and-click investigation plus rail-shooter action
  • CHAOS and Vera Vale
  • Time-hopping cases
  • Touch and mouse aiming
  • Cover-to-reload shooting

Controls

Desktop

Click to look, talk and shoot. Hold cover to reload when the firefights start.

Phone

Tap to shoot, hold cover to reload. Full screen plus landscape is the intended view.
