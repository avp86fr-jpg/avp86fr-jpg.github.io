METAWINNERS — MOTO RIDES
Portable HTML5 publishing package / V6.2

Upload this ZIP as an HTML Game on itch.io, or extract it into an HTTPS static
web root. index.html belongs at the root. All game libraries, artwork, models,
fonts, sounds and music are local; no build step, wallet or backend is required.
Preserve relative paths, filename case and all credits/license notices.

See ITCH-QUICKSTART.txt for upload settings and ITCH-STORE-COPY.txt for listing
copy and attribution. Test the draft on your target devices before release.

The 25 riders are custom stylized adaptations of original MetaWinners portraits.
Native regression and source geometry checks do not establish browser/device
frame rates. Browser/itch iframe and physical-device verification remain pending.
