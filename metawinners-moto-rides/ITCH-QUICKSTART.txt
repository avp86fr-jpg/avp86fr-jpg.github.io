METAWINNERS / MOTO RIDES — ITCH.IO UPLOAD

UPLOAD THIS FILE
metawinners-moto-itch-html5.zip

1. Create or edit your itch.io project. Choose HTML Game.
2. Upload this ZIP and select This file will be played in the browser.
3. Recommended launch: Click to launch in fullscreen. Keep Click to Play on.
4. Alternatively use a 1280 x 720 embed with Fullscreen Button enabled.
5. Check your draft on desktop and your target phones. Enable Mobile Friendly
   after confirming touch controls and audio in the itch preview. Landscape
   gives the best view. Tap a game control to enable audio.
6. Copy the included store text and use artwork from the marketing package.

The archive has index.html at its root and all runtime assets included. Upload
the ZIP directly: do not wrap it in another folder or upload the marketing ZIP.
No wallet, build step, API key or runtime CDN is needed.
