TITLE
MetaWinners — Moto Rides

SHORT DESCRIPTION
25 riders. Four circuits. Full-contact motorcycle racing with heavy metal, weapons, and Overdrive. No wallet required.

DESCRIPTION
Ride. Strike. Rule.

Pick your MetaWinner and take a matching bike onto the Vegas Strip, Casino City, Miami, or the Desert. Thread traffic, lean through corners, and fight for the opening with a bat, chain, or wrench.

Clean passes, near misses, and strikes build Flow. Fill the meter and hold Nitro for five seconds of Overdrive. Race seven rivals, hunt takedowns in Elimination, or beat the clock in Time Attack.

25 distinct riders and motorcycle liveries. Four circuits across twelve districts. Three race modes. Keyboard and touch controls. Heavy metal music. Local best scores. No wallet connection.

CONTROLS
A/D or Left/Right: steer. S/Down: brake. Space: strike. Q/E: strike left/right.
Shift: Nitro / ready Overdrive. C: camera. Escape/P: pause.
Touch: touch the pad, slide to lean, release to straighten. Brake, Hit, Nitro.
Acceleration is automatic. In the garage, swipe horizontally to orbit and
vertically to scroll. Use the +/− buttons to zoom.

BROWSER NOTES
Requires a current WebGL2 browser with hardware acceleration. Landscape is
recommended on phones; Performance graphics reduces rendering load. Tap to
start audio. Scores stay in the current browser when local storage is available.
Single-player; no online multiplayer or gamepad support is claimed.

MODEL AND MEDIA NOTE
